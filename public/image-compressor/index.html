Why Compress Images?

Large photos slow down websites, eat phone storage, and get rejected by forms with file-size limits. Compressing shrinks the file while keeping it looking great:

  • Job & exam forms — many Indian government and job portals cap uploads at 20–100 KB. Our compressor gets you under the limit.
  • Faster websites — smaller images mean faster page loads and better Google rankings.
  • Easy sharing — send photos over WhatsApp or email without hitting size limits.
  • Save storage — shrink your photo library by 60–80% with no visible quality loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CompressKit really free? +
Yes — 100% free, no sign-up, no limits, no watermark. It runs entirely in your browser so there are no server costs to pass on to you.
Are my photos uploaded to a server? +
No. All compression happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never leave your device — that's why it's private by design.
How do I compress an image to a specific size like 100 KB? +
Upload your image, then lower the quality slider until the live preview shows your target size. For most photos, 60–80% quality hits 100 KB with no visible difference.
Which format should I choose? +
WebP gives the smallest files at the same quality — best for websites. JPG is the safest for forms and older systems. PNG keeps transparency for logos and screenshots.
Will compression ruin my image quality? +
At 80% quality, the difference is virtually invisible — but files shrink 60–80%. Drop to 60% for tiny files where perfect quality doesn't matter (thumbnails, chat sharing).
Can I compress multiple images at once? +
Yes! Select multiple files and each gets compressed with your settings. Download them one by one from the file list.
